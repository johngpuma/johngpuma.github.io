Featured project

The Puma

A Catan 1v1 bot trained from scratch by self-play on one gaming PC.

It never saw a human game. It learned only by playing against itself, about four million games in two days of compute, and now beats Catanatron's search bot 197 games out of 200 without searching at all.

  • Model: a 279K-parameter graph neural network over the board's corners and edges, predicting the best move and who's winning.
  • Training: PPO from self-play; a shaping reward faded to zero by iteration 200, leaving winning as the only goal.
  • Systems: 11 CPU workers feed one GPU through shared memory, batching about 130 positions per call.

CS 229 · 2026

Beyond Pip Counting: Interpretable ML for Catan Openings

A learned model beats the standard heuristic at predicting who wins from the opening: AUC 0.73 vs. 0.54 on ~100K simulated games.

  • 52 features (resource scarcity, turn order, opponent overlap), split by game so no game leaks across train and test.
  • Compared logistic regression, XGBoost, an MLP and SVMs; scored with AUC and Brier score for calibration.
  • Explained with SHAP and distilled into 8 human-readable rules that keep 62% of the model's edge.
